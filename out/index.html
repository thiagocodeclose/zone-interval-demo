The Five
Zones

Each zone triggers a different physiological response. Train them all, in the right sequence, and you build a complete athlete.

1
Active Recovery
50–60% MHR

Warm-up and cool-down. Steady breathing, minimal effort. Primes your cardiovascular system.

2
Aerobic Base
60–70% MHR

Building your engine. Fat-burning zone. You can hold a conversation — barely.

3
Threshold
70–80% MHR

The sweet spot. Aerobic and anaerobic converge. Maximum sustainable effort.

4
Lactate
80–90% MHR

Pushing past comfortable. Lactic acid accumulates. Short bursts. Big results.

5
Max Effort
90–100% MHR

Everything you have. 10–30 seconds. This is where transformation happens.
